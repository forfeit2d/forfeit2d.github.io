Squat Form & Safety Checklist

Protect your knees and lower back with proper biomechanics.

1. Flat Feet & Tripod Base

Drive down through your whole foot (heels, big toe, pinky toe). Never allow your heels to lift off the ground.

2. Knees Track Over Toes

Push your knees gently outward in the direction of your second toe. Avoid letting knees collapse inward (valgus cave).

3. Controlled 2-Sec Tempo

Lower yourself down under full control for 2 seconds. Pause briefly at parallel before driving straight back up.
